About Sama-R Shop

Our Story

Every great business starts with a dream, and ours is built on hard work, determination and a passion for serving our community. Based in Kuils River, we are a small, locally owned store dedicated to providing our customers with quality products, friendly service and a shopping experience they can trust.
We believe that being a small business is our strength. It allows us to connect with our customers, understand their needs and give every person who walks through our doors the attention they deserve. Through dedication, consistency and a commitment to doing our best, we continue to grow while staying true to our roots.

Who We Are

We are a hardworking and dedicated team that takes pride in what we do. As a local store in KuilsRiver, we understand the importance of building strong relationships with our customers and being a reliable part of the community.
We believe in treating every customer with respect, honesty and kindness. Whether you are shopping with us for the first time or are one of our regular customers, we strive to make every experience a positive one.

Our Mission

Our mission is to provide our customers with quality products, excellent service and great value for their money. We aim to make shopping convenient, enjoyable and accessible while building lasting relationships with the people we serve.
We are committed to working hard, improving every day and going the extra mile to ensure our customers leave satisfied.

Our Vision

Our vision is to become a trusted and recognised local store, known for reliability, quality and outstanding customer service.
We aspire to grow our business, reach more customers and create new opportunities while maintaining the personal touch and dedication that make us who we are.

Our Values

The principles that guide everything we do:

-Hard Work: We believe success comes through dedication, consistency and giving our best every day.
-Honesty and Integrity: We believe in being transparent, trustworthy and fair in every interaction.
-Customer Satisfaction: Our customers are at the heart of our business. Their happiness and trust matter to us.
-Quality: We strive to offer products that meet our customers' expectations and provide good value for money.
-Reliability: We aim to be a store our customers can depend on.
-Community: We are proud to be part of Kuils River and value the relationships we build with the people around us.
-Growth: We believe in learning, improving and finding better ways to serve our customers.
